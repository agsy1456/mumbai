Amchi Mumbai

  • City of Dreams: It is famously called the "City of Dreams" because it attracts people from all over the country seeking success and fortune.
  • Financial Hub:. The city serves as India's primary economic centre and houses major financial institutions like the Bombay Stock Exchange.
  • Home of Bollywood: Mumbai is the heart of the Hindi film industry, popularly known as Bollywood.
  • Rich History: Formed originally from an archipelago of seven islands, the city was formerly known as Bombay before being officially renamed Mumbai.
  • Famous Landmarks: Iconic sights include the historic Gateway of India waterfront arch and the scenic coastal road known as Marine Drive.
  • Popular Food: Local street food favorites include spicy Vada Pav and Pav Bhaji.
  • Busy Transit: Millions of commuters rely daily on its fast-paced and extensive local train network.
